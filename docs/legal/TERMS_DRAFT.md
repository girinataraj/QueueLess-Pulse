## Intended access and use

QueueLess Pulse is currently an invited technical demonstration for evaluators, project reviewers and testers. The demonstration is not approved for critical service delivery, real customer operations or general commercial use.

## Actual functionality and limits

The application issues anonymous queue tickets, estimates waits from configured durations, supports staff transitions and records business-scoped history. Estimates are approximate; there is no contractual queue time, availability, emergency-service or delivery guarantee. Recovery links are bearer viewing capabilities and must remain private. Invitations are shared manually, expire after 24 hours, can be accepted once and grant no automatic service assignment.

Network connectivity is required for live operation. The intended free demonstration hosting may sleep or expire under the provider's plan. No service-level agreement, compliance certification, payment/subscription terms or guaranteed data-retention period is offered by this draft. Historical data persists under current implementation; account deletion/export/recovery are not self-service capabilities.
